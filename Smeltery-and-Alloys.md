# Smeltery and Alloys

Build a 5 × 5 Seared Brick floor and a perimeter wall above it. The four outside corners of the floor and each wall layer are optional. Leave the center 3 × 3 open. Put the Controller, a Drain and a lava-filled Seared Tank in the wall. Place the Faucet outside the Drain and a Casting Table or Casting Basin immediately below the Faucet.

Put both metals in the Controller input. The smeltery melts one item, then advances to the next eligible slot. For example, copper in slot 1 and tin in slot 9 both get processed while the stacks stay where you placed them. The selected molten metal controls pouring. Choose the alloy recipe separately to mix it.

A one-layer starter build needs 21 Seared Bricks blocks for the floor and nine for the wall, plus the Controller, Drain, Seared Tank, Faucet, and Casting Table. Budget 152 Seared Brick items, one Furnace, and one Glass for this build.

## Alloys, reusable casts and the Tool Forge

Make a reusable gold part cast by placing its stone pattern on the Table and pouring the listed Gold amount. Standard part-cast creation uses two Gold Ingots. Pour the desired metal into the cast, cool it and collect the part; the gold cast remains.

Alloy batches: 3 Copper + 1 Tin -> 4 Bronze; 1 Gold + 1 Silver -> 2 Electrum; 2 Iron + 1 Nickel -> 3 Invar. Steel uses 1 molten Iron plus 1 Coal and 1 Blaze Powder from solid input -> 1 Steel.

Select the alloy and enable Auto alloy. It mixes when the required molten metals and any solid catalysts are available. Select the finished alloy to pour it into a suitable cast.

Cast a Head, Rod and Binding and assemble the supported modular pickaxe/hammer in the Tool Forge. Materials may differ between parts. The atlas includes the supported combinations.

Forge modifiers are not machine boards. Reinforcement Plate adds durability; Efficiency Module applies Efficiency II; Fortune Module applies Fortune I to a compatible pickaxe. Hold a modifier, sneak-use the Forge and select a tool. Restrictions on incompatible/equal-or-higher enchantments still apply.
