# Essence and Equipment

## Tool roles, metal armor and vein mining

Pickaxes mine stone/ores; axes mine wood and strip logs; shovels dig soil and make paths; hoes till suitable soil. Paxels combine pickaxe/axe/shovel mining and applicable stripping/path actions.

Crooks use leaf bonus rolls. Hammers crush supported blocks. Sickles and scythes harvest nearby mature crops, with the scythe covering the larger area. Swords provide combat damage. Tool wear and enchantment compatibility depend on the tool.

Sneak-mine with a supported pickaxe, axe, shovel, paxel, hammer or crook to mine connected matching blocks. Cap: 64 total, or earlier when a finite tool breaks. Release Sneak or switch tools to stop queued work.

Crook leaf rolls and hammer conversions happen per block in a vein. Unbreakable Supremium tools still obey the 64-block cap. Fortune is not a universal multiplier for every scripted crushing or machine output.

Ordinary metal armor uses normal crafting-table armor shapes with its own ingots. Modular tools use the Forge. Essence gear uses a different tier-upgrade route in the next chapter.

## Essence armor: the complete five-tier progression

Sieve Dirt for Inferium Essence and Sand for Prosperity Shards. Make an Essence Crafting Seed with one Wheat Seed and four Prosperity Shards in a cross. Surround that seed with four Inferium Essence in the same cross. Plant the resulting Inferium Essence Seeds and interact with mature plants to harvest without removing the seed.

At a NORMAL CRAFTING TABLE, craft an Infusion Crystal: four Prosperity Shards in the corners, four Gold Ingots on the edges, one Diamond in the center. Putting those nine items into the Essence Tinkering Table does not craft the crystal.

Craft the Essence Tinkering Table from two Prosperity Shards, one Inferium Essence, five Stone and a Crafting Table. Rows: Shard/Essence/Shard; Stone/Crafting Table/Stone; Stone/Stone/Stone. Place and open it. It needs NO POWER.

Insert four lower essence plus the already-crafted crystal into its input slots. Unpause and wait four seconds. It batches up to 64 output and retains the crystal, spending one use per infused output. Chain: Inferium -> Prudentium -> Intermedium -> Superium -> Supremium, always 4:1.

The crystal lasts 1,000 infused outputs. A Master Crystal has unlimited uses: normal crystal in the center, four Prosperity Ingots on the edges and four Supremium Essence in the corners.

Craft ingots at a normal table: Iron Ingot + four Prosperity Shards -> Prosperity Ingot. Prosperity Ingot + four Inferium Essence -> Inferium Ingot. Each later ingot uses one previous-tier ingot + four matching next-tier essence. All are shapeless.

Craft initial Inferium armor in normal armor shapes: helmet 5, chestplate 8, leggings 7, boots 4; 24 Inferium Ingots total. Upgrade one previous armor piece + four NEXT-tier ingots in the Essence Tinkering Table. Every complete set upgrade uses 16 next-tier ingots.

Full-set bonuses: Inferium is base armor; Prudentium gives water breathing; Intermedium gives step assist; Superium prevents fall damage; Supremium prevents fall damage and grants native flight. Wear all four matching pieces. Higher sets do not automatically inherit all earlier bonuses. Supremium gear is unbreakable.

For all armor through Supremium, plan for 24 Inferium Ingots, then 16 each Prudentium/Intermedium/Superium/Supremium Ingots. Make EXTRA lower-tier ingots for those higher ingots: ingots already consumed by armor cannot be reused. Gear upgrades preserve names, enchantments and proportion of durability used.

Upgrade seed tiers at a crafting table with the previous seed plus four of the next essence in a cross. They yield 1/2/3/4/5 INFERIUM per harvest, never higher essence directly. Build the farm first, then infuse its output for gear.

Native flight requires Education features and Cheats. Equip the full Supremium set in Survival/Adventure, wait for ready, then double-tap Jump. Jump rises, Sneak descends, sprint boosts speed; double-tap stops flight. No flight charm is needed. Removing a piece revokes and resets active flight, restoring the original Survival/Adventure mode. Creative and Spectator flight are left alone.

## Essence charms and watering cans

Blank Essence Charm: one Prosperity Ingot + four Supremium Essence. A finished charm uses the blank, two Supremium Essence and its specialty ingredient. Put it with a compatible Supremium piece in the Essence Table to install one charm. Charmed gear plus Flint removes and returns the charm.

Armor charms include Speed, Night Vision, Water Breathing, Fire Resistance, Resistance and No Fall. Tool charms include compatible Strength, Fortune, Silk Touch, Efficiency and Mining Area effects. One charm per item. See the atlas for every compatible target and ingredient.

Craft an Inferium Watering Can from its ingots and a Bucket. Later can recipes use the previous can. Use on water to fill, then on a crop/soil to water the nearby area. Higher tiers have larger areas.

Remaining water percentage appears while held, with exact water/capacity in the tooltip. An accepted watering action spends one unit immediately, even on mature crops. The first successful plant growth is included; additional plants spend another unit each. Refill when empty.

A short hand stream and target splashes animate watering. Effects are throttled and spend no extra water. Switching the selected slot cancels remaining queued work. Watering a mature plant does not create an additional harvest.
