# First Island

## Make your first Cobblestone

1. Turn Oak Logs into planks and sticks. Save a sapling and keep the original dirt for replanting.
2. Craft a Wood Crook. Break normal oak leaves with it for saplings and a chance at Silkworms. Use a Silkworm on intact oak leaves, wait for the infestation to spread, then crook the infested leaves for String.
3. Craft a Sieve from four Oak Planks and four String in any arrangement. Make a regular Composter from seven wooden slabs in a U.
4. Use eight spare saplings on an empty Composter. Wait for the green material to finish composting and collect the Dirt.
5. Use Dirt on the Sieve. The loading click is the first of six interactions. Craft four ordinary Pebbles into one Cobblestone. Repeat until you can build a Kiln and a Furnace.

## Make water, clay, and lava

- **Water.** Set an empty Composter under open sky to catch rain, or add four leaf blocks collected with shears. An empty bucket draws water when the Composter is full.
- **Clay and buckets.** Hammer Cobblestone into Gravel, Gravel into Sand, and Sand into Dust. One Dust in a full ordinary-water Composter makes one Clay Block. Break it into Clay Balls. Craft three Clay Balls into an Unfired Clay Bucket and fire it in a Furnace.
- **Lava.** Use Cobblestone on a Kiln. One piece melts into one bucket of lava. Collect it with an empty bucket. The Kiln runs without Watt power.
- **Renewable Cobblestone.** Build a fireproof trough. Arrange a water source, a one-block-deeper catch pit, an open mining cell, and a lava source in that order. Mine the Cobblestone in the meeting cell.
- **Pumped water.** Place an empty Composter on top of a Moisture Collector. Supply lava to the collector and use a Fluid Pump to move the collected water into a dedicated water Tank.

## Stone Barrel and Witch Water

The **Stone Barrel stores lava only**. Each recipe uses 100 mB of lava. Add one Glowstone Dust for End Stone, one Redstone Dust for Netherrack, or one Water Bucket or Clay Water Bucket for Obsidian. The Obsidian recipe returns the empty bucket. Sieve Sand or Dust for Redstone and Glowstone Dust.

For **Witch Water**, sieve Sand or Red Sand for Ancient Spores and use them on Dirt to make Mycelium. Place a full ordinary-water Composter directly over Mycelium. Wait for Witch Water, then add one Sand to make Soul Sand.

For **Netherite**, hammer Netherrack into Crushed Netherrack. Sieve it for Netherite Scrap. Even the basic mesh can roll Scrap at 1% per completed input. Craft four Scrap with four Gold Ingots into one Netherite Ingot.

For **Grout**, combine one Clay Ball, one Sand, and one Gravel to get two Grout. Smelt Grout into Seared Brick for the smeltery.
