# First Island

## First island: wood, saplings, string and cobblestone

Protect the initial tree and saplings. Expand before harvesting everything so drops do not fall into the void. Make a crafting table, sticks and a Wood Crook. Replant before using the last sapling.

Use a crook on normal leaves for its species-matching bonus sapling roll and Silkworm roll. Shared bonus rolls are 15% sapling and 3% Silkworm per leaf, in addition to native leaf loot. Better crooks mainly improve speed/durability; they do not change those bonus odds.

Use a Silkworm on oak leaves to infest them. Infestation spreads to neighboring oak leaves. Harvest infested leaves for string and establish the sieve/mesh progression. Sneak vein mining applies the same rolls separately to every leaf.

Compost plants into dirt and sieve dirt for pebbles. Four ordinary pebbles craft Cobblestone; specialty pebbles make their named stone. Mulch has been removed. Archived mulch-based dirt/clay instructions do not apply.

## Composting, collected water, clay and mycelium

Feed accepted plant material to the composter. Green filling composts over time into ready dirt; collect the finished dirt after conversion. Keep partial batches separate from extraction.

Place a composter over a Moisture Collector to collect ordinary water. A Fluid Pump and purple Transfer Pipes can move it into a compatible water tank. Full or incompatible tanks wait without deleting the source fluid.

A composter on Mycelium is reserved for Witch Water. The ordinary-water pump route does not steal that brewing water. Sieve Sand or Red Sand for Ancient Spores, then use the spores on Dirt to obtain Mycelium in Survival.

The Stone Barrel and fluid/dust routes provide early conversions. Use the atlas for exact clay and material recipes; a manual barrel recipe and a later processor recipe may obtain the same item in different ways.

## Hammers, sieving and mesh progression

Use a hammer for supported crushing conversions. The early chain starts Cobblestone -> Gravel -> Sand -> Dust. Other supported rocks have their own outputs. A better hammer does not automatically improve sieve odds.

Insert a supported block into a Sieve and interact until its six work clicks complete. Faster clicking progresses its animation faster. Manual results eject as physical items, so provide a safe collection platform.

Use Iron, Diamond or Netherite Mesh on the sieve to upgrade it. Basic/String, Iron, Diamond and Netherite apply x1, x1.25, x1.6 and x2 to independent base rolls, capped at 100%. Sneak-use empty-handed to remove an upgrade and return the mesh.

Dirt has a 5% base Inferium Essence roll: 6.25%, 8% and 10% with higher meshes. It is not guaranteed per block. If all independent rolls fail, the sieve chooses a weighted fallback item. The later drop tables therefore show base independent rolls, not exclusive final probabilities.

## Fragments, crushed ore, powder and ingots

Fragments from crops, crushed material from sieving and powder from processing are distinct items. Follow each material through its combine/smelting recipes in the atlas rather than treating every ore-looking item as equivalent.

Fresh raw ore processing can have an ore bonus, but recycled ingots must not multiply forever. Four Iron, Gold or Copper Powders combine into an INGOT, not raw ore. Pulverizing a finished ingot and recombining its powder is neutral recycling.

The Smeltery accounts for powder, ingots and fresh raw ore using different molten quantities. Four powders represent one ingot. Supply machine fuel/power and free output space before assuming a processor has stalled.

## Material recipes: End Stone and other progression blocks

END STONE: place a Stone Barrel. Supply at least 100 mB of lava, then insert 1 Glowstone Dust to produce 1 End Stone. A lava bucket holds 1,000 mB and supports ten conversions. For automation, connect a lava tank through the fluid network and insert Glowstone Dust through a Transfer Node or MV Link. Extract End Stone from the barrel output. No Watt power is needed by the barrel itself.

NETHERRACK: use the same Stone Barrel with 100 mB lava plus 1 Redstone for 1 Netherrack. A hammer breaks Netherrack into Crushed Netherrack; sieve the crushed material for Nether resources. Consult the linked sieve tables for exact rolls and mesh multipliers.

OBSIDIAN: insert 1 Water Bucket into a Stone Barrel containing at least 100 mB lava. This pack produces 1 Obsidian and returns the empty bucket. A Clay Water Bucket also works and returns a Clay Bucket. These are pack-specific conversion costs.

MYCELIUM: obtain Ancient Spores by sieving the supported sand inputs, then use Ancient Spores on Dirt. Mycelium is required below the water-filled composter for Witch Water. See the sieve tables for the current chance for your input and mesh.

SOUL SAND: put a water-filled composter above Mycelium and wait until its water becomes Witch Water. Insert Sand; the conversion uses the 1,000 mB water fill and produces Soul Sand. Refill with water and wait for the next batch. Use a moisture collector for renewable water and item/fluid transfer for automation.

CLAY AND GROUT: make Dust by hammering Sand, then add Dust to a water-filled composter for Clay. Use Clay with Sand and Gravel to craft Grout; the added-block recipe atlas gives exact ingredient alternatives and quantities. Cook Grout into Seared Brick, including in the Magma Furnace, then craft the smeltery blocks.

STEEL: build a valid smeltery and provide lava fuel. The Steel alloy uses 1 Iron + 1 Coal + 1 Blaze Powder to produce 1 Steel. Select the stored metal to cast it through a Drain and Faucet into a suitable cast. Selecting Steel does not itself supply missing alloy ingredients.

NETHERITE: hammer Netherrack into Crushed Netherrack and sieve it with a mesh that enables the required Netherite-stage drop. Gold Nuggets and Quartz are separate rolls and do not guarantee a Netherite roll. The sieve tables show the exact input and mesh requirements. Smelt Ancient Debris if that is your drop, then combine 4 Netherite Scrap with 4 Gold Ingots for 1 Netherite Ingot.

ESSENCE EQUIPMENT: start with Prosperity Shards and Inferium from the documented sieve/crop routes. Grow essence crops to maturity before harvesting. Use the infusion progression and tiered ingot recipes in the complete essence recipe section, then follow each armor or tool recipe. The seed is not itself an ingot; repeated mature harvests supply its ingredients.
